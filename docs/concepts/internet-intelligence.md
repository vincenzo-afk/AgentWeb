# Internet Intelligence

"Internet Intelligence" is the category AgentWeb positions itself in, deliberately broader than "search API" or "scraping API." It reflects the idea that AI is only one customer segment — the same platform can serve research teams, ecommerce operators, journalists, analysts, security teams, founders, compliance teams, and product organizations that need timely, trustworthy, and inspectable internet-derived intelligence.

An Internet Intelligence Platform turns web intent into grounded outcomes using search, browser execution, memory, graph reasoning, monitoring, and transparent citations — rather than exposing isolated retrieval primitives that the user must orchestrate themselves.

Why this framing matters:

- It signals that the product handles *strategy*, not just *access*.
- It positions AgentWeb against a wider, higher-value competitive set (agent infrastructure, enterprise intelligence tooling) rather than only search/scraping vendors. See [research/competitive-landscape.md](../research/competitive-landscape.md).
- It sets the expectation that results come with evidence, not just answers. See [explainability.md](explainability.md).
