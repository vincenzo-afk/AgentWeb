# Non-Goals

These are permanent exclusions, not just later phases:

- **Not a general-purpose consumer search engine.** AgentWeb is not competing to be a Google-style front door for arbitrary human queries.
- **Not a system of record.** AgentWeb does not replace internal databases, data warehouses, or knowledge bases — it produces internet-derived evidence, not a store of a customer's own private data.
- **Not a content platform.** AgentWeb does not host or republish scraped content as a product; extraction exists to support synthesis and monitoring, not content redistribution.
- **Not a legal or compliance authority.** Trust scores and citations are decision-support signals; AgentWeb does not certify compliance or make legal determinations (see [docs/security/compliance-notes.md](../../docs/security/compliance-notes.md)).
- **Not a replacement for human judgment in high-stakes decisions.** Explainability exists so humans can verify, not so the system's output can be trusted blindly.

See [PROJECT_SCOPE.md](PROJECT_SCOPE.md) for phased (temporary) scope boundaries.
