# Business Requirements

## Revenue model

Usage-based pricing tied to [retrieval mode](../../docs/concepts/retrieval-modes.md) depth and monitor frequency, consistent with the cost drivers in [docs/research/economic-model.md](../../docs/research/economic-model.md).

## Target segments

AI/agent developers, product/developer teams, business operations teams, research/analyst teams, and consumer-automation builders — see [docs/research/user-segments.md](../../docs/research/user-segments.md) for the full breakdown.

## Business-level success conditions

- Demonstrate that outcome-first orchestration measurably reduces integration cost vs. a fragmented tool stack for at least one high-value workflow (the MVP wedge — see [docs/roadmap.md](../../docs/roadmap.md) Phase 0).
- Establish a defensible learning moat via accumulated task/strategy intelligence (see [SUCCESS_METRICS.md](SUCCESS_METRICS.md)).
- Support enterprise/compliance-sensitive adoption via explainability guarantees (see [docs/guides/enterprise-rollout.md](../../docs/guides/enterprise-rollout.md)).

## Constraints

Must not require customers to pre-select tools/connectors to get a useful result (violates the core [outcome-first thesis](../../docs/concepts/outcomes-over-tools.md)).
